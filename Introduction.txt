Gaming platforms have went from being hardware , metal, heavy   arcade machines to being fully digital and accessible to anybody. Gaming isn’t an expensive way to kill time anymore, due to rise of personal computers , mobile devices and consoles. There were times when kids could only dream and save for months , in hopes of one day buying a console. Now, with the gaming industry moving to cloud, and game development becoming so accessible, anyone can play games , also without having to go anywhere. And with constant breakthroughs and evolution from arcade machines, to simple consoles , to personal, programmable computers, and now being fully digital, the demand for resources also rise. Now, PC’s have became so powerful and revolutionized, being able to do things that people couldn’t imagine of happening in millions of years, they use lots of energy and they need a place for their digital cloud, which is being supported by data centres around the world. On average , a laptop uses 30 to 70 watts of electricity, and large desktops and gaming computers use between 200 and 500 watts of electricity . Using a computer for 8 hours per day will use about 12.2 killowat -hours of electricity per month and 146 killowat – hours of electricity per year , according to Jacob, M. (2024) . Now imagine hundreds of millions users with gaming computers all around the world using 146 killowat of electricity per year.  Its an enormous amount of energy consumed, and this is without counting the amounts used by data centres , or other appliances. Second large problem of the evolution of gaming platforms , Is the data centres. They need tons of water to cool, from article written by Miguel, Y. : only 3% of Earth’s water is freshwater, and only 0.5% of all water is safe for consumption. A medium sized data center can consume up to roughly 110 million gallons of water per year for cooling purposes, which is the equivalent to the annual water usage of approximately 1000 households , and this is only a medium sized. A large sized data center uses up to 5 million gallons of water per day , or about 1.8 billion gallons annually , usage equivalent to a town of 10,000 to 50,000 people. So while , yes , evolution of gaming platforms made spending time more fun then ever, it became more comfortable and accessible, but it also became more demanding. We burn through our resources like crazy without realizing it, and soon , if we keep going we might cause irreversible damage.
